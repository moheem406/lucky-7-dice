  ### Sprint 1
1) Goal: [Describe the result you want.]
The player can choose a number and predict what the dice will roll bigger than 7 equal than 7 or less than 7 if the player
predict correct he will get a life if prediction is wrong he loses a life , if the player lost all life's the game is over
or else keep making high score

2) Mission : The player need to continue winning to get more lives and keep beating the High score to reach highest

3) Thing the player can change: The player can change the highest score back to 0 just by clicking Reset High score button
    and keep changing its prediction or guess!!.

4) Messages: The player will get messages in the board that it won the prediction or lost the prediction and also the total number which randomly came and also he gain life or loss life and even when game is over.

5) friendly game: Player can play this game with friends and do friendly bets.

6) Technologies: HTML CSS and Javascript windows Emojis and Google sysmbols were used in this game.

7) Future planning: In future we can make it more advance by animations sounds and effects.
